## Habits that make it stick

1. **Say it out loud.** Read the "Say it like a pro" lines aloud (🔊 helps) and answer every Interview Corner question in your own words. This is how you become someone who can speak about networking, not just recognise it.
2. **Flashcards daily, even on busy days.** Five minutes keeps the streak alive and the memory fresh.
3. **Drill subnetting until it's boring.** Goal: any /16 to /30 question in under 30 seconds.
4. **Labs without hints, the second time.** Redo every mission from memory a week later.
5. **Boss battles at 90%+ before exam week.**
6. **Trade only demo, and journal every trade** with its setup and emotion. Don't consider real money until your written graduation criteria are met.

## Before you book the CCNA

- [ ] Every Network chapter boss defeated
- [ ] Three mock exams in a row at 82% or higher (100 questions)
- [ ] All Packet Lab missions and tickets completed without hints
- [ ] Subnetting drills: 20 in a row correct
- [ ] Downloaded and read Cisco's official **CCNA v2.0 exam topics** PDF (exam from 3 Feb 2027)
- [ ] Practised on real IOS or Packet Tracer as well (you have `.pka` files from ComNet)
